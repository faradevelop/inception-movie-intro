Level 03

The Snow Fortress

Ten years · The vault

A fortress hospital carved into the snowfields. Somewhere below the ice, a father's secret waits inside a vault that opens like a safe.

Your mind is the scene of the crime.

The team doesn't steal anymore. This time they must do the impossible — not take an idea out of a mind, but plant one so deep it grows as if it had always been yours. Extraction, inverted.

An idea is the most resilient parasite known to the mind. Once it takes root, it defines you. To build one, you descend through layers of dream — each level deeper, slower, and harder to leave than the last. Six sleepers. Ten hours of flight. One idea, folded into a stranger's inheritance.

“An idea is like a virus. Resilient. Highly contagious. And the smallest seed of it can grow to define — or destroy — you.”

— Dom Cobb
